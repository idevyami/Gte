# ENTITY_000 — 02 · World & Lore Bible

Phase 0 documentation. This bible locks the setting's facts, the canonical proper nouns, the four deliberate contradictions the slice plants, and the five fundamentals of THE DESIGN. Canon rule: the slice reveals, never explains. Anything written here as subtext must remain subtext on screen.

## 1. The City of Ash

The City of Ash is a civilization where a single principle — everything must have a purpose — became a religion, then a law, then a government, and finally reality itself. Nobody in the city experiences this as history; they experience it as weather. Ash falls eternally, fine and gray, and no one questions it because ash is what remains when purpose has been extracted from things.

The city's population is fading. Not dying — fading: faces lose detail, names stop being remembered, and the census stones need recarving more often. The streets are administratively perfect and increasingly empty. Where people vanish, their purpose sometimes outlives them, drifting loose until something collects it.

The player's route climbs from beneath: The Vessel Chamber (a vertical iron womb), up through The First Door, The City of Ash, The Contradiction, The Chapel of the Pale Saint, The Watching, The Engine Sanctum, and finally The Reliquary. Each district is a stratum of the same idea at a different stage of decay — civic, sacred, industrial, memorial.

## 2. THE DESIGN: One Structure, Two Readings

THE DESIGN is simultaneously the city's religion and its administrative system. This is the central ambiguity of the game and it is never resolved. The two readings share every outward form:

| Form | Religious reading | Administrative reading |
|---|---|---|
| THE DESIGN | The divine order of purposes | The master records system |
| MEASURERS | Priesthood | Clerks and auditors |
| CENSORED objects | Sin made absent | Deleted records / removed assets |
| ANCHORS | Shrines where one prays and is kept | Checkpoints where state is written |
| THE HEART | The god at the center | The engine at the center |

The duality is delivered through tone, not twist: a Measurer blesses a doorway with the same gesture they use to stamp a form. ANCHORS both pray and checkpoint — one animation, two true descriptions. The player is never told which reading is correct, and the ending does not arbitrate.

## 4. The Five Fundamentals of THE DESIGN

The fundamentals exist as recited text (believers), posted law (plaques), and internal data (OBSERVE). Each has a belief reading and a law reading, exactly as THE DESIGN itself does.

| Fundamental | As belief | As law |
|---|---|---|
| 1. All that exists must serve a purpose | Purpose precedes creation | Existence requires a registered purpose |
| 2. All purpose must be measured | The MEASURERS witness worth | Audits are mandatory and periodic |
| 3. What is purposeless is without reality | The CENSORED were never worthy | Unregistered objects are deleted |
| 4. What is believed by many is measured true | Collective faith creates saints | Consensus data outweighs single records |
| 5. What deviates must be corrected | Correction is mercy, not punishment | Maintenance of the record |

A sixth fundamental is whispered to exist. It is never stated in the slice — not on any plaque, not in any readout, not by any NPC. The Penitent's contradictions gesture around its absence. The withheld sixth is the game's most deliberately planted hook for content beyond Phase 0.

## 5. The Four Deliberate Contradictions

The slice plants exactly four contradictions between the world's accounts of itself. They are the load-bearing evidence that reality here is maintained, not given. Two are mechanically fixable (contradiction fix, 5 Consistency each); two can never be resolved and simply accumulate dread.

| Contradiction | Where | Form | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| The plaque against the readout | Act 3–4, DOOR_029 tollgate and the Undercity door | A plaque states the gate has opened for no one since the Third Enumeration; OBSERVE reads the door's memory of openings — the numbers disagree | Fixable: the Undercity contradiction puzzle door opens when the accounts are reconciled (−5) |
| The room counted twice | Act 4–5, Undercity census stones | Chamber 114 is counted twice in the census; the physical room exists once; at stage 2+ its decor visibly duplicates in sympathy | Never resolvable; escalates visually with Consistency stages |
| The street that is not | Act 5–6, a Measurer NPC | The NPC remembers Vellum Street with sensory detail — the smell of its bakeries, the pitch of its bells; no such street exists in geometry or record | Never resolvable; at stage 3 the NPC notices you noticing |
| The records against the mural | Act 6, Archive Loft vs Chantry | The Archive's records enumerate 817 failed entities; the Chantry mural depicts a different number of candle-shrines, and the mural has been corrected by hand | Fixable: the Archive memory contradiction can be reconciled (−5), which is what exposes the hidden platform path at stage 6 |

Contradiction rules: a contradiction is never flagged by UI. It is discovered by reading plaques against OBSERVE data, or by hearing an NPC insist on something the geometry denies. The game trusts the player to notice; the MEMORY fragment system exists to reward noticing.

## 6. Daily Texture

Life in the city is bureaucratic liturgy. Toll machines collect purpose-debts at gates; their plaques quote the fundamentals. MEASURERS process queues of the fading, stamping purposes onto people who are losing the definition to hold them. Children are measured early; Null Children drift where classification failed. Nobody is cruel; cruelty would require someone to enjoy this, and enjoyment itself has been measured out of the city long ago.

ANCHORS stand where the city still functions: kneeling markers with a bowl of ash and a thin system-cyan vein of light. Using one is a single gesture that reads as prayer and as a save — the light pulses, the world's state is written, and the phrase ANCHOR ACCEPTED appears in the same typography as every other system message. The first communion at each anchor restores +12 Consistency; below 60 Consistency the restoration is withheld — RESTORATION WITHHELD, RECORD TOO UNSTABLE — mercy has a filing threshold.

The ash itself is the city's quietest confession: the residue of everything CENSORED. It falls eternally because correction is eternal. This is never stated. It is staged: ash thickens near censorship sites, and the Reliquary's ash is deepest of all.

## 7. What the Slice Must Never Do

- Never explain THE DESIGN's origin, mechanics of divinity, or whether the religious or administrative reading is "true."
- Never state the sixth fundamental, even in endgame files.
- Never resolve The Penitent. It appears, contradicts, and departs.
- Never use the word "glitch" in-fiction. Corruption is experienced as correction: objects are CENSORED, not broken.
- Never confirm the player's origin beyond the numbering: 818 predecessors exist as records; what made them, and what makes you, is withheld.
