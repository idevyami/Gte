# ENTITY_000 — 05 · Narrative & Theology Bible

Phase 0 documentation. This bible locks the narrative law of the slice: the two questions and their placement, the reveal ladder, the voice rules for The Penitent, the canonical text inventory, and the ending specification. The governing rule of all writing on this project: reveal, never explain.

## 1. The Two Questions

ENTITY_000 is built on exactly two questions, and their placement is law:

**Q1 — the central question:** If you were created for a purpose, do you have the right to reject it?

**Q2 — the secondary question:** What happens to a thing nobody believes in?

**Placement law:**

| Rule | Q1 (purpose) | Q2 (belief) |
|---|---|---|
| Who asks it | The world asks the player; no NPC ever voices it directly | The world asks it about others, silently |
| Where it lives mechanically | The player's own record: purpose, flags, the boss's evolving purpose field | Belief values, the Pale Saint, the Martyr, the fading population |
| First planted | Act 1, the moment RESTORE THE WORLD prints as an order | Act 5, the Pale Saint sequence |
| Stated verbatim | Exactly once: the final screen of Act 9 | Never stated verbatim |
| Answered | Never | Never — only demonstrated in reverse by the Martyr |

The two questions braid: everything that exists in the City of Ash holds its place by purpose or by belief, and the player is the one thing whose existence is argued in both courts at once. The ending's power comes from refusing to close either case.

## 3. The Penitent: Voice Rules

The Penitent is the slice's one recurring unexplained entity. Rules are absolute:

1. It appears at least twice (a full sighting in Act 6; at least one earlier peripheral appearance).
2. It speaks only in contradictions — every line must be formally self-opposing.
3. It is never explained, never named in-fiction by NPCs, and never enters combat.
4. It never lies. Its contradictions are the most literally true statements in the game.
5. Its typography differs from all other dialogue: slightly corrupted glyphs, no speaker label.

Canonical Penitent lines (locked; the full set is three to five lines across the slice):

- "I have always been sorry. I was never here."
- "You will remember me incorrectly. That is how I remain."
- "The street I mourn was never built. I walk it every day."
- "Do not believe me. I am the only thing they all agreed on."

The Penitent's final line, if the player has collected the memory contradictions, is delivered at the Archive Loft and concerns the sixth fundamental by refusing to state it: "There is a sixth. It is the one they never wrote down. That is how you will know it is the true one."

## 4. Canonical Text Inventory

All in-fiction text belongs to one of five classes. Wording below is canonical; plaques render in carved serif small-caps, system messages in the terminal face.

**Plaques (civic, posted law):**

- Tollgate plaque, Act 3: "THIS GATE HAS OPENED FOR NO ONE SINCE THE THIRD ENUMERATION." (The OBSERVE readout disagrees: 41 openings. The first planted contradiction.)
- Census stone, Act 4: "CHAMBER 114. CHAMBER 114." (One room, counted twice. Never resolvable.)
- Fundamentals posts: the five fundamentals, verbatim from the World & Lore Bible, one per post.

**Ritual text (Chantry, recited):**

- Believer chant: "Measured, measured, measured be. What is measured cannot leave."
- Ward consecration: "Hold what is held. Believe what is believed."

**Inscriptions (memorial):**

- The Martyr's revealed inscription, Act 8, exact: "ENTITY_000_001 — FAILED — REPURPOSED"
- The monument, Act 9, carries the same inscription, unchanged, persistent.

**System messages (cold cyan, terminal face):**

- "OBJECTIVE: RESTORE THE WORLD" (Act 1; the opening order)
- "CONSISTENCY: {value}" and "CORRECTION IN PROGRESS" (stage transitions and CENSOR events)
- "ANCHOR ACCEPTED" (save)
- "PROPERTY MODIFIED: {entity}.{property}" (every manipulation; the world files its objection)

**OBSERVE data text:** memory fields carry one-line records written as testimony ("Opened for the toll collector. Opened for the Penitent. Not for you."). Memory fields are the only prose the world addresses to the player directly.

## 5. Ending Specification (Act 9)

Act 9 takes place in the Reliquary after the Martyr's death, with the monument persistent and the arena emptied of threat. The sequence is locked:

1. The player approaches the monument; the inscription is legible and unchanged.
2. The system reveals, in its own typography and pacing, the count: 817 predecessors, failed; you are ENTITY_000_818.
3. One system line states the stake: the world you restore may not include you.
4. The game then prints the central question, verbatim, and stops:

   > If you were created for a purpose, do you have the right to reject it?

5. No answer options. No epilogue cards. The question holds on screen until the player exits; the title returns with the question ghosted into its background.

Ending law: the ending is a question, not a lore dump. Nothing in Act 9 may resolve the Design's duality, the Penitent, the sixth fundamental, or the player's origin. The only new information is the count and the stake — everything else the player already earned on the ladder. The final emotional target is not triumph or grief but jurisdiction: the question of whether refusal is a right the player can claim, left deliberately in the player's hands.

## 6. Writing Rules for All Slice Text

- Contradictions are never flagged; they are stated plainly by two authorities that disagree.
- NPCs speak in administrative liturgy; warmth leaks through procedure, never around it.
- The Design is never explained by any character who would know; those characters are unavailable, fading, or bound.
- Damage, death, and correction are described in record language, never in violence language.
- Every proper noun is used exactly as locked in the World & Lore Bible, capitalization included.
